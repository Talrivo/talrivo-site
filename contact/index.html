Contact FAQ

Questions before contacting TALRIVO

What is the official TALRIVO email for product inquiries?

Use sales@talrivo.com for gaming headset model details, TWS earbud samples, audio product RFQs, logo and packaging discussions, and product document questions.

What information should a buyer include in the first message?

Include the target market, sales channel, selected model or product category, intended use, estimated quantity range, sample timing, branding scope and documents to check.

Can buyers use this page for TWS044F and Earclip09S sample questions?

Yes. Buyers can use the contact form for TWS044F in-ear TWS, Earclip09S open-ear TWS, TWS model comparison, private-label sample requests and packaging review.

Can buyers use this page for G926, G938 and G941 sample questions?

Yes. Buyers can use the contact form for G926, G938, G941 and other TALRIVO gaming headset sample questions, model comparison, packaging review and RFQ preparation.

How can buyers confirm a TALRIVO quotation?

TALRIVO does not publish fixed product prices on the website. A quotation should be confirmed through an RFQ because model version, quantity range, accessories, packaging, branding scope and destination-market requirements can change the commercial scope.

Where can a buyer submit the TALRIVO website RFQ form?

Use the direct inquiry form on the Contact TALRIVO page or the RFQ form on the home page. Both collect the buyer, market, product, quantity, timing and project requirements needed for follow-up.

Can buyers contact TALRIVO through LinkedIn, WhatsApp or WeChat?

Yes. LinkedIn, WhatsApp and WeChat can support initial communication. Model versions, samples, quotation scope, packaging and document requirements should also be recorded in the website form or email.

Should sample, customization and quotation questions be sent together?

They can be included in one focused message when the model, market, quantity and buying stage are clear. Buyers can also use the sample request, customization and RFQ guide pages to prepare each step.
