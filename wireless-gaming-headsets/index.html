Product FAQ

Common questions before product review

What should buyers check before choosing a 2.4G wireless gaming headset?

Check the intended market and sales channel, connection and receiver direction, microphone structure, wearing comfort, product images, sample plan, branding needs and packaging questions.

What is the difference between 2.4GHz and Bluetooth gaming headsets?

A 2.4GHz headset normally uses its included receiver, while Bluetooth uses device pairing. Buyers should compare the intended device scenario, connection setup, sample behavior and whether tri-mode use is required.

Can TALRIVO support private-label wireless gaming headset projects?

Yes. Logo, color and packaging direction can be discussed after the buyer selects a suitable model and reviews the sample version.

What should an importer include in a wireless gaming headset RFQ?

Include the destination market, sales channel, preferred connection type, model interest, quantity range, sample timing, logo needs, packaging direction and document questions.

Why should buyers review a sample before confirming packaging?

The approved model and sample determine the receiver or cable set, logo area, accessories, user information and packaging direction that should be discussed for the project.
