Before sampling

Six details to match to your sample

Version
Use the exact model, frame, color and revision. Do not borrow specifications from a wired or wireless namesake.
Connection
Identify the receiver, cable and port. A USB plug may carry audio, charging or lighting; check its function.
Microphone
Inspect the boom and connector, then record speech on the sample. Appearance does not establish voice clarity.
Fit
Review cushion shape and adjustment, then try the sample on intended users. A wearing image is not a comfort test.
Color and logo
Confirm the finish and proposed logo area on the selected version before approving artwork.
Included items
Match the receiver, microphone, cables and printed materials to the agreed packing list, not just a photo.

FAQ

Common product evidence questions

What can product images show?

Visible shape, color, connectors, microphone structure and accessory layout. Match every image to the selected model version.

What needs a physical sample?

Sound, microphone behavior, fit, controls and device compatibility need sample checks. Photos alone do not establish battery life or latency.

Which TALRIVO pages should I use?

Start with the model page, then use the microphone, packing and sample-review guides for the functions and accessories you need to verify.

What should I send with a sample question?

Send the model or product link, target device and market, plus the photo detail or function you want checked.

Can workshop photos prove capacity or certification?

No. They show process context, not factory size, capacity, certification or completed test results. Request separate supporting evidence.
