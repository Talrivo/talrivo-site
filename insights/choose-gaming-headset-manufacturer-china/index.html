Before you choose a manufacturer

Four questions worth asking before you order a sample

You do not need a long presentation to start. Ask for answers you can check, then keep them alongside the sample and agreed specification.

1. Who will make the headset and handle changes?Ask who prepares the sample, follows production and deals with a problem. Confirm the legal company named on the quotation and later contract or invoice. Review the selected assembly footage, but do not treat a workshop video as proof of capacity or inspection results.
2. Is the video showing the version I will receive?Ask for the exact connection version, receiver, microphone and accessory list. G935 wired and wireless versions must be confirmed separately. The G938 setup video shows a different model; it is not a G935 performance test. Use the blank sample review sheet to record your own results.
3. Does everything in the photo come in the box?Ask for a written packing list. Separate the standard receiver, cables and microphone from optional adapters or other extras. Agree logo, color, manual language and packaging changes using the customization scope table; do not assume a photographed accessory is included.
4. Do the documents cover this exact model?Read the holder name, model list, date and report references, then check the selected configuration and destination market. Our published CE/RED and RoHS references list specific models, not the whole range. G936 is not listed in those two documents. A shared model code alone does not establish coverage for a different version.

A useful first message: "We are looking for a gaming headset for [devices] in [market]. Can you show us the sample version, included accessories and documents to review before we choose?" You can send this as a short question without preparing a full RFQ.

Sample workflow

Connect sample review, packaging and documents before approval

A supplier comparison is strongest when the buyer can trace how a model moved from page evidence to sample feedback and then to RFQ.

1. Record product evidence

Capture model code, current images, receiver or cable direction, microphone structure, accessory list and source of each claim.

2. Evaluate the sample

Review appearance, fit, controls, connection setup, voice use, accessories and packaging before approving a version.

3. Check microphone use

Practical checks should cover boom position, keyboard noise, fan noise, mute behavior and voice naturalness during calls or games.

4. Confirm packaging and accessories

Match headset, microphone, receiver, cables, manual, box direction and labeling questions with the selected model.

5. Prepare document questions

Connect destination-market document questions to the exact headset configuration rather than a broad category claim.

6. Send a focused RFQ

Share model codes, target market, quantity range, sample result, branding scope, packaging direction and unresolved questions.

FAQ

Gaming headset supplier buyer questions

How should importers choose a gaming headset manufacturer in China?

Start with factory identity evidence, model evidence, current product images, sample traceability, microphone and connection setup, packaging scope, destination-market document questions and a complete RFQ. Compare suppliers only after the quoted sample versions are comparable.

What factory evidence should buyers request before choosing a gaming headset manufacturer?

Ask for the legal company name used on documents, a current factory or workshop video or live walkthrough, clear production or sample-preparation responsibility, and model-level document scope. Treat factory size, capacity and certification claims as unconfirmed until documents or live evidence are verified.

Can workshop photos alone prove capacity, certification or a test result?

No. Workshop photos can support questions about visible process areas, but capacity, certification, inspection scope and individual test results should be confirmed separately for the selected model and project.

What product evidence should a gaming headset supplier provide before sample approval?

Useful evidence includes model identity, current product images, receiver or cable direction, microphone structure, accessory list, packaging reference, specification source and clear notes on which claims still require sample confirmation.

What should buyers include in a gaming headset RFQ?

Include target market, sales channel, model codes, connection direction, target devices, quantity range, sample timing, logo or packaging needs, accessory questions and destination-market document requirements.

Which TALRIVO headset models can buyers review first?

Buyers can start with G938 for a refined tri-mode direction, G941 for a lead tri-mode model with videos, G936 for a lightweight fabric-cushion direction, G926 for a 50mm RGB wireless direction, G940 for another wireless sample path, and G935 for a clean wireless model with published video reference.

Does TALRIVO publish gaming headset prices on the website?

No. B2B quotations depend on selected model version, quantity range, branding, packaging, accessories, destination market, documents and sample plan.

What should buyers avoid when comparing headset manufacturer claims?

Avoid relying on unsupported factory-size, capacity, certification, latency or price claims. Ask for current evidence, model-level documents and physical sample confirmation before approval.
