RFQ checklist

Eight points to include in the first message

This checklist helps TALRIVO reply with a focused model direction instead of a broad catalogue answer.

1. Target country or region

Tell the supplier where the product will be sold. This affects document discussion, packaging language, sales channel and product positioning.

2. Buyer type and sales channel

Identify whether the project is for distribution, retail, online store, private-label brand, promotion or sample comparison.

3. Product direction

State whether you need wired RGB, 2.4G wireless, Bluetooth, tri-mode wireless, lightweight office-gaming crossover or a mixed product line.

4. Model shortlist

If you have a target style, share reference models or TALRIVO model interests such as G938, G941, G940, G926, G935 or G936 so the discussion starts from a real product direction.

5. Quantity range

Provide an estimated range instead of asking for one universal price. B2B quotation depends on configuration, quantity, packaging and project details.

6. Logo and color needs

Explain whether you need product logo, cable tag, packaging logo, custom color direction or neutral branding for first sample review.

7. Packaging direction

Clarify retail box, neutral box, user manual language, accessory set and whether product images or listing material will be needed later.

8. Sample and document timing

Share when samples are needed and which destination-market document questions should be discussed after the model version is confirmed.

Better first message

Turn a vague quote request into a useful product brief

Too vague

“Please send your best gaming headset price.” This does not tell the supplier which model, channel, market, quantity or branding direction to review.

More useful

“We are preparing a private-label wireless gaming headset range for [market]. Please recommend 2.4G USB receiver or Bluetooth models suitable for [channel], with sample options, logo area and packaging discussion.”

Even clearer

“Target quantity is [range]. Target devices are [PC/console/laptop]. We need samples first, then retail packaging and logo discussion after model approval. Please advise which product details and document questions should be confirmed.”

Common mistake

Do not start with packaging before selecting the product

Packaging and logo work depend on the selected headset structure. The logo area, accessories, receiver, cable, manual, ear cushion shape and product photos can change by model. For a cleaner private-label project, choose a model direction first, review samples second, and confirm packaging after the approved product version is clear.

Model firstChoose the headset type and visual direction before deep packaging discussion.
Sample secondThe sample should confirm appearance, comfort, microphone position and product finish.
Branding thirdFinalize logo, color, packaging and document questions after the sample version is confirmed.

FAQ

Private-label gaming headset RFQ questions

What should a private-label gaming headset buyer prepare before requesting a quote?

Prepare the target market, buyer type, sales channel, model direction, connection type, quantity range, logo needs, packaging direction, sample timing and document questions.

Do private-label buyers need exact model numbers before contacting TALRIVO?

No. Buyers can start with a product direction such as wired RGB, 2.4G wireless, Bluetooth or tri-mode wireless gaming headset, then review suitable models and samples.

Which model pages can help prepare a private-label headset RFQ?

Buyers can review G938, G941, G940, G926, G935 and G936 pages before sending model interest, connection needs, sample questions and branding details.

Why should logo and packaging discussion come after model selection?

Logo area, color options, accessory set, user information and packaging direction depend on the selected model and approved sample version.
