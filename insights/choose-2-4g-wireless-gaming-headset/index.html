Quick answer

A 2.4G wireless headset should be reviewed as a complete product story

Low-latency positioning matters, but product selection should also include microphone structure, battery direction, comfort, visual design, packaging and sample review.

Latency and receiver setup

Confirm whether the model is positioned around 2.4G wireless use, receiver pairing and low-latency gaming scenarios.

Microphone and communication

Review boom microphone design, position, flexibility and whether the headset fits team voice communication.

Battery and wearing comfort

Check battery story, charging direction, headband structure, ear cushion size and long-wear use case.

Product images and sample review

Use model images to compare shape and finish, then confirm details through sample review before final decisions.
