Questions about TWS057

Does TWS057 have a user-replaceable case battery?

The drawings show an internal battery compartment and a separate cover, but they do not establish user-replacement support. Request the current construction and service guidance for the selected version. Do not open or service a case based on this illustration.

What can I confirm from the TWS057 display drawing?

It shows a numeric display design, not verified display behavior or remaining-time accuracy. Check the actual indicators, charging state and port on a complete sample, and obtain matching earbud views before approving artwork.

Is the 13mm TWS057 driver automatically better than TWS042S?

No sound-quality ranking is established by these records. TWS057 lists a 13mm driver while TWS042S lists 6mm; compare tuning, fit and the same audio passage on samples instead of treating the larger diameter as a proven improvement.

Does TWS057 deliver 30 hours from one earbud charge?

About 30 hours is a listed catalogue figure, not a new playback test. Confirm single charge or charging-case-assisted use, whether ANC is on or off, volume and call use before adopting runtime wording for your product listing.
