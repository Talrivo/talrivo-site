Product FAQ

Questions about TWS042S

How does TWS042S differ from the TWS057 ANC reference?

TWS042S lists a 6mm driver, 30mAh earbud battery and 600mAh case. TWS057 lists a 13mm driver, 35mAh earbud battery and 300mAh case. These are configuration differences, not a sound-quality or endurance ranking; compare the approved samples under the same conditions.

Can the round TWS042S faceplates carry my logo?

The supplied renderings show placeholder brand artwork on the circular faceplates. Ask about the actual printable area, printing method, finish and artwork approval for your selected version before treating the illustration as a production specification.

Does the listed TWS042S 35-hour figure mean one earbud charge?

The catalogue lists about 35 hours without defining the complete test conditions. Confirm single charge or charging-case-assisted use and whether ANC is on or off. Do not publish the figure as verified continuous earbud playback.

Which TWS042S finish and accessories should be confirmed?

Agree the faceplate and case colors, artwork, charging cable, tips if applicable, manual and packing list for the selected version. The illustrated finish is not a confirmed color or accessory offer. Pictured parts are not automatically included.
