Before sample approval

Check the connection your buyers will use

The video is a physical sample demonstration, not a universal compatibility or microphone-quality test. For Bluetooth, confirm the device name and pairing procedure on the selected version, then test with the USB receiver removed so the audio route is unambiguous.

  • Record the exact phone, OS version, receiver and headset firmware.
  • Check audio playback and microphone input separately; music playback alone does not verify voice-chat performance.
  • Verify the power, M button, volume and reconnection behavior against the supplied instructions.
  • Match the microphone, receiver, adapters and cables to the approved packing list.
