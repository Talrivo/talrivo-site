Before ordering samples

Three questions worth asking after you watch

Is this the same headset I would order?

Confirm the model, connection version, microphone and receiver or cable set. A shared model name or similar housing does not mean the electronics and accessories are identical. Compare wired gaming headsets and wireless models separately.

What should I check on the finished sample?

Start with headband adjustment, bracket movement, earcup alignment, cushion fit and visible finish. Then check audio, microphone and controls on your target devices. Use the blank sample review worksheet to record results; the video is not a completed test report.

What can I change for my brand?

Agree the base version first, then discuss logo, color, manuals and packaging. Changes to the structure, electronics or functions need a separate review with the factory. See the OEM and private-label customization scope.
