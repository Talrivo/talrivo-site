## LinkedIn Post

2.4G and Bluetooth serve different headset sourcing requirements.

For an importer or private-label buyer, the connection label alone is not enough:

- 2.4G normally uses a dedicated USB receiver for a gaming-focused PC or console setup.
- Bluetooth supports direct pairing with phones, tablets and laptops.
- A documented tri-mode model can combine 2.4G, Bluetooth and wired use.

Before approving a sample, check the receiver type, target devices, microphone route, battery wording and package contents for the selected version.
