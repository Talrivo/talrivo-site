# TALRIVO Multilingual and Regional GEO Plan

This plan keeps regional expansion controlled. Do not machine-publish many translated pages at once. Start with short, useful regional pages based on real buyer intent.

## Priority Order

1. English global pages.
2. Spanish pages for Mexico, Argentina, Uruguay and wider Latin America.
3. Portuguese pages for Brazil.
4. Russian pages for Russian-speaking buyers.
5. Indonesian and Vietnamese pages for Southeast Asia.
6. Thai, Japanese and Korean summaries after product evidence is stable.

## Page Types To Translate First

Start with pages that answer practical buying questions:

- gaming headset category page;
- wireless gaming headset category page;
- sample request page;
- RFQ guide;
- G941 model page;
- one comparison article.

Avoid translating every page immediately. Regional pages should be useful entry points, not thin copies.

## URL Structure

Recommended simple structure:

- `/es/` for Spanish.
- `/pt-br/` for Brazilian Portuguese.
- `/ru/` for Russian.
- `/id/` for Indonesian.
- `/vi/` for Vietnamese.
- `/th/` for Thai.
- `/ja/` for Japanese.
- `/ko/` for Korean.

Each translated page should include:

- canonical for its own URL;
- `hreflang` links for available language versions;
- localized title and meta description;
- localized FAQ questions;
- links back to product pages and contact path.

## Regional Positioning

Spanish:

- practical sourcing tone;
- emphasize model selection, sample request and packaging direction.

Portuguese Brazil:

- clear product category and RFQ information;
- avoid overly technical wording unless the page is a product specification page.

Russian:

- direct specification and product comparison style;
- strengthen model details, connection type and sample process.

Southeast Asia:

- clear price-value positioning;
- simple model comparison and fast sample discussion path.

Japan and Korea:

- more polished, product-led wording;
- be careful with claims and keep design/spec language precise.

## Translation Workflow

1. Choose one English source page.
2. Confirm the buyer intent and target country.
3. Translate the page, not just the words.
4. Localize the title, meta description and FAQ.
5. Add `hreflang` after at least two language versions exist.
6. Submit new URLs in sitemap and Search Console.
7. Track impressions by country before adding more pages.

## Quality Rule

Do not publish a regional page unless it has:

- a clear buyer question;
- a real product or category link;
- a sample request or contact path;
- a localized meta description;
- no unsupported claims.
