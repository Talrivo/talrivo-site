# TALRIVO 4-Week SEO/GEO and LinkedIn Content Plan

Purpose: build low-cost natural traffic by turning buyer questions into website articles, LinkedIn posts and internal links.

Rules:
- Do not publish public prices.
- Do not invent product specs, certificates, reviews or ratings.
- Use real product images, model pages, sample questions and buyer scenarios.
- Every website article should have one LinkedIn post and one clear website link.

## Weekly execution checklist

1. Publish or update one website article.
2. Add the article to sitemap.xml and llms.txt.
3. Share the URL in one LinkedIn post.
4. Request indexing in Google Search Console.
5. Check Search Console after 7-14 days for impressions, not only clicks.
