## Two Alerts to Configure Once Signed In

- `"TALRIVO" -site:talrivo.com`
- `"talrivo.com" -site:talrivo.com`

Use https://www.google.com/alerts with the intended business Google account.
Inspect existing alerts first to avoid duplicates. Suggested settings: at most
once a day, automatic sources, any language and region, all results. Verify the
receiving address on screen before saving. Do not monitor common model numbers
or the name Lily Li alone. Alerts discover matching new Search results, not all
web mentions, and do not create backlinks by themselves.

## Qualify Before Contacting

1. Read the actual page. Confirm a relevant, accurate mention of TALRIVO or an
   original TALRIVO resource, not a namesake or unrelated scraped page.
2. Check whether a working source link already exists. An owned LinkedIn or
   YouTube post is distribution, not an independent endorsement.
3. If a useful source link is missing, contact the responsible editor once with
   the specific relevant guide. Never promise a reciprocal link, buy ranking
   credit, or demand a keyword-rich anchor or removal of nofollow attributes.
4. Record the outcome. A mention is not a link; a link is not a click; a click is
   not an inquiry. Do not treat Alerts email count as an SEO result.
