## Publishing rule

For each keyword group:

1. Match the page title and first paragraph to the search intent.
2. Add product evidence such as images, receiver detail, microphone, logo area or sample notes.
3. Add internal links to one category page, one model page and one contact or sample page.
4. Reuse the topic as one LinkedIn post and one sales follow-up message.

## October 22 acquisition check

- These keyword groups are working intent hypotheses, not verified search volumes or current rankings. Do not create a new near-duplicate URL for each wording.
- Keep exact model-code queries separate from TALRIVO brand queries, supplier-intent queries and connection-problem queries. A model-code impression is not proof of purchasing intent.
