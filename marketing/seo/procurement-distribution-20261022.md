The headset lights up. So why is there no sound?

On some wired gaming headsets, USB powers the lighting while the 3.5mm plug carries sound and microphone signals. A USB-audio headset uses a different audio path.

Before ordering headsets for your brand, confirm the selected version, target device and whether a CTIA splitter is needed. For the configuration described in our guide, the splitter is a separate accessory and is not included in the headset unit price.
