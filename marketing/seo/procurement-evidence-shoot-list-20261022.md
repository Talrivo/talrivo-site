# Procurement evidence shoot list

Prepared October 22, 2026. These are requested materials, not proof that a feature, test or customer relationship exists. Prioritize the first three groups; do not delay useful material until every item is ready. No public prices or certificate uploads are requested.

## First priority: three practical shoots

### 1. G938: a complete Bluetooth pairing sequence

- One continuous horizontal clip, approximately 45-90 seconds. Show the headset version and device first.
- Start with the headset disconnected. Show the button operation, the actual Bluetooth device name appearing, selection and the connected status. Keep the phone screen readable and hide personal notifications.
- Show playback and, if supported, microphone use in the intended app. Do not treat a connected icon or phone music as proof of microphone operation.
- If switching to the 2.4GHz receiver, show the mode change and what actually happens. Do not imply simultaneous audio unless it is demonstrated and confirmed.
- Send a note with the phone/PC model, OS, headset version, receiver or adapter, app and observed result. Failed or unsupported combinations are useful too.
- Intended use: replace the current pairing-evidence gap in the setup guide and G938 page, then make a question-led social clip. Existing footage does not clearly show completed Bluetooth pairing.

### 2. G935 wired: identify USB lighting versus audio

- Confirm this is the wired version before shooting; do not reuse a wireless model's connection claims.
- Show USB-A and 3.5mm plugs close up, with the cable traced to the headset.
- Record three states on the same compatible device: 3.5mm only, USB only, both connected. Show what actually works for playback, microphone and lights in each state.
- If using a computer with separate headphone and microphone sockets, show the exact CTIA headset splitter and state that it is a separately supplied accessory, not part of the headset unit price for the described configuration.
- Preserve the raw sound. A microphone meter supports an input check but does not by itself establish speech quality; include a short voice recording when possible.
- Intended use: real test evidence for the existing USB guide. Current lifestyle/connector images are illustrations, not completed connection tests.

### 3. G936 and G938: exact packing list and material details

- Photograph each selected version separately on a clean table: headset, microphone, receiver, adapter, each cable, manual and box. One overhead full set plus 2-3 readable close-ups per model.
- Identify standard contents, optional accessories and illustration-only items in a short accompanying note. A photo alone is not a universal packing list.
- G936: close up fabric texture, seams, headband, cushion recovery after a gentle press and visible controls. Do not infer heat resistance, lifespan or comfort ratings from appearance.
- G938: close up receiver connector, USB-C adapter, microphone socket, buttons and volume wheel. Include receiver markings only when approved for publication.
- Intended use: model-specific accessory and comfort answers, product images and sample discussions without making untested compatibility claims.

## Second priority: trust and original manufacturing evidence

### 4. Current assembly and actual inspection

- Separate short clips: assembly station, one actual functional inspection, packing and outgoing preparation. Show what the operator does, not just a line of machines.
- For a test, capture model/version, device or instrument, operation and actual result. Record test conditions; do not stage a pass or infer certification from equipment.
- State whether each scene belongs to our own manufacturing operation or an authorized partner. Confirm filming and publication permission, including recognizable staff, screens and customer packaging.
- Legal company names previously kept private remain private. Send an approved publication copy rather than asking AI to invent a factory image or modify a certificate.
- Intended use: improve the manufacturing and quality-review page with new original evidence; no unsupported capacity or certification promises.

### 5. A real logo/packaging customization example

- One approved base product, artwork placement, approved sample and final retail contents. Note exactly what changed and what stayed standard.
- Use a customer brand or logo only with publication permission. Otherwise use a truthful anonymized completed project or a clearly labeled illustrative mockup; do not mix them.
- No customer order value, capacity, delivery promises or confidential files are required.
- Intended use: one substantive customization example, not multiple thin pages or an invented brand case.

## Capture and delivery

- A phone is enough: original 1080p or better, horizontal 16:9, steady hands/tripod, clear light and readable screens. Capture an extra vertical take only if convenient.
- For evidence clips, keep raw audio and uncut actions; no music, beauty filters or speed changes. Edited social versions can be made later from the originals.
- Send the original files as files rather than compressed chat previews where practical.
- Suggested filename: G938_BluetoothPairing_device_date.mp4. Include a short note: model/version, device/OS, connection/accessories, tested operation, observed outcome, restrictions and publication permission.
- Keep private phone notifications, buyer contacts, serial labels, barcodes and unapproved customer logos out of the public copy. Do not alter factual test results or certificate fields.
