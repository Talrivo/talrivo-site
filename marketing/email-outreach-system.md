## GEO Workflow

Every useful customer email question can become content.

Examples:

- Buyer asks about 2.4G wireless headset options: add a FAQ or article section.
- Buyer asks for sample process: improve the sample request page.
- Buyer asks how to compare wired and wireless models: link or update the comparison article.
- Buyer asks about logo and packaging: add evidence to the GEO evidence log.

Record strong buyer questions in:

`marketing/geo-evidence-log.md`
