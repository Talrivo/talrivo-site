# TALRIVO GEO Content System

Use this workflow to turn one TALRIVO product topic into website content, LinkedIn content and Facebook content without rewriting the message from scratch.

## Core method

For GEO, the website should provide the stable source. Social posts should repeat the same topic and link back to the source page. This helps search engines and AI systems connect TALRIVO with a clear product category, product question and official profile.

## Source package

Prepare these items before publishing:

- product model or category;
- target question customers ask;
- 3 to 5 product facts;
- 1 clear product image;
- optional product video;
- related website page URL;
- short proof point such as sample note, feature check, comparison point or common customer question.

Do not invent inquiry numbers, customer names, performance claims or test results. If a result is not verified, use neutral wording such as "product review note", "model comparison" or "sample discussion point".

Record every usable proof point in `marketing/geo-evidence-log.md` before turning it into an article or social post.

Use `marketing/social-publishing-calendar.md` to decide which website topic should be pushed to LinkedIn and Facebook first.

Use tracked URLs from `marketing/conversion-tracking-notes.md` or create new UTM links before publishing social posts.

## MiKe-style publishing standard

Use this standard for each topic:

1. Pick one buyer question or search intent.
2. Match the landing page title, opening paragraph, image evidence and internal links to that intent.
3. Use real product evidence: product images, model details, sample notes, customer questions or comparison points.
4. Add a practical next step: product page, sample request, product brief or contact path.
5. Reuse the same topic on LinkedIn and Facebook with a link back to the website source.

## Website article

Recommended structure:

1. Clear title based on a customer question.
2. Short answer in the first paragraph.
3. Product comparison or selection criteria.
4. Internal links to related TALRIVO product pages.
5. FAQ section using direct questions.

Example topic:

- What to check before choosing a 2.4G wireless gaming headset.
- How to compare wired and wireless gaming headsets.
- How RGB gaming headsets fit a gaming audio collection.
- What to prepare before asking for logo and packaging options.

## LinkedIn post

Use a professional and concise format:

```
Many customers compare wired RGB gaming headsets with 2.4G wireless gaming headsets before choosing a product direction.

For TALRIVO, the first checks are latency positioning, microphone structure, comfort, product image clarity and packaging direction.

We summarized the selection points here:
https://talrivo.com/insights/compare-wired-wireless-gaming-headsets/
```

## Facebook post

Use a more visual and direct format:

```
Wired or wireless gaming headset?

TALRIVO compares the key points: connection type, microphone, comfort, RGB appearance and product positioning.

Read the guide:
https://talrivo.com/insights/compare-wired-wireless-gaming-headsets/
```

Add a clear product image or short product video when posting on Facebook.

## Reuse map

- One website article becomes one LinkedIn post and one Facebook post.
- One product video becomes one short website note, one LinkedIn caption and one Facebook caption.
- One customer question becomes one FAQ answer, one article section and one social post.

## Measurement loop

After publishing, review:

- Search Console impressions and queries;
- inquiry email source context;
- LinkedIn and Facebook comments or messages;
- questions that can become new FAQ or article sections.

Keep the topic if it produces a real search query, useful social comment or buyer conversation. Otherwise, improve the page title, product evidence or internal links before publishing similar content again.
