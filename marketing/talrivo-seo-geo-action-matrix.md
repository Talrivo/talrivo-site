## Page-Level Fixes To Check Later

For each important page, check:

- title is specific and buyer-intent focused;
- meta description is 120 to 160 characters;
- H1 matches the search topic;
- first paragraph gives a direct answer;
- product images have descriptive alt text;
- page links to related product/category/sample pages;
- page has a clear inquiry CTA;
- no public price is shown;
- no fake review/rating schema is used.

## GEO Answer Pattern

Every new article should include this pattern near the top:

```text
Question:
What is the buyer trying to decide?

Short answer:
Give a direct answer in plain English.

Why it matters:
Explain the business reason for importers, distributors or OEM/ODM buyers.

What to check:
List 4 to 6 practical evaluation points.

TALRIVO direction:
Connect the answer to a product category or model.
```

This makes the page easier for Google, AI search tools and buyers to understand.
