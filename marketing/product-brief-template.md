# TALRIVO Product Brief Template

Use this template when replying to buyers who ask for price, samples, catalogue or logo and packaging options.

## Short version

Please share:

- product category or model interest;
- target market and sales channel;
- estimated order quantity;
- whether you need samples first;
- logo, color, packaging or manual language needs;
- target price level if available;
- sample timing or launch timing.
