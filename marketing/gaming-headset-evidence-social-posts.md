## LinkedIn post

Before selecting a gaming headset model, product teams should review more than a single product name.

For TALRIVO gaming headsets, the useful checks are:

- connection type: wired, 2.4G wireless, Bluetooth or tri-mode;
- receiver or cable detail;
- boom microphone structure;
- headband and ear cushion shape;
- color direction and logo area;
- sample and packaging questions.
