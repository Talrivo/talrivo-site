Before publishing

Four checks for a model-specific product listing

Approve the product information before the page design. One model code alone does not settle the connection, accessory or performance wording.

Images and the shipped versionIdentify the model, color, receiver, cable and microphone shown. A lifestyle scene, rendering or exploded drawing is not automatically a photo of the final packing contents.
The actual audio connectionCheck which plug or receiver carries playback and microphone audio. Use the USB audio or lighting guide before treating a USB plug as proof of USB audio.
Battery and noise-cancellation wordingKeep runtime conditions with the figure. Separate wearer-side ANC from microphone-side noise reduction using the ANC and ENC comparison.
One approved packing listMark included, optional and excluded items using the packing checklist. Do not copy every accessory in a promotional image into the box-contents claim.

Product FAQ

Common questions

Can a supplied product image prove which accessories are included?

No. Match each pictured receiver, adapter, cable, microphone and printed item to the approved packing list for the selected version. Separate included, optional and excluded items before writing the listing.

Can a creative image prove ANC performance or battery life?

No. A rendering or lifestyle illustration can explain a product direction, but it is not a performance test. Confirm the selected configuration and the conditions behind each noise-cancellation or runtime statement.

What should I confirm before copying headset specifications into a listing?

Confirm the exact model and version, audio connection, receiver or cable, microphone behavior, battery wording and packing contents. Keep the approved sample reference with the listing so that claims are not borrowed from a similar model.

Does this checklist guarantee marketplace approval?

No. This is a product-information review, not marketplace approval or a platform-policy assessment. Check the current requirements of your selected platform and destination market separately before publishing.
