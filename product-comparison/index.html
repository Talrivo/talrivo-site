How do G935, G938, G940 and G941 differ?

G935 uses a 40mm driver and 500mAh battery; G938 uses a 53mm driver and 1000mAh battery; G940 emphasizes dual-driver construction and a 400mAh battery; G941 uses a 50mm driver, 1000mAh battery and tri-mode positioning.

How do B7, B9 and B10 differ?

B7 and B10 list 500mAh batteries and dual-feed ANC direction, while B9 lists Bluetooth 5.3 + EDR and a 300mAh battery. B10 has the longest listed music time.
