FAQ

Common questions

How do G938, G941, G936 and G935 differ?

G938 uses a 53mm driver and 1000mAh battery; G941 uses a 50mm driver, 1000mAh battery and tri-mode use; G936 uses a 40mm driver and 800mAh battery; G935 uses a 40mm driver and 500mAh battery.

Which TALRIVO models should buyers compare first?

Start with G938 or G941 for tri-mode wireless directions, G936 for a 40mm 2.4G route with fabric-cushion direction, and G935 for a cleaner 2.4G wireless sample path.

Should buyers choose from specifications alone?

No. Use the data to shortlist models, then confirm comfort, controls, microphone or ANC behavior, accessories, packaging and documents on samples.

Yes. Share destination market, sales channel, quantity range, target product role and evaluation timing.
