Product FAQ

Common questions

Should I request Spanish or Portuguese headset packaging?

Specify the destination country and the language needed for each box, manual and label. For Brazil, identify the Portuguese artwork you want reviewed; for Spanish-language channels, supply the approved wording for that market. Confirm translation and artwork responsibilities before production approval.

Can one wholesale quotation cover several headset models?

You can send a mixed shortlist, but ask for quantity and terms per SKU. Keep the receiver, USB-C adapter, cables, branding, packaging and delivery scope itemized. A price or MOQ for one configuration should not be assumed to apply to another.

Does a Brazil document cover all Latin America destinations?

Do not assume so. Review the selected product and destination with the importer or a qualified local adviser. A reference to Brazil's ANATEL process is not proof that a TALRIVO model is approved, and this page does not establish approval for any other country.

What should a Latin America distributor send first?

Send the destination country, retail or online channel, product categories, target devices and quantity per SKU. Include packaging language, accessory needs, sample timing and document questions. TALRIVO can then discuss a shortlist and quotation against that scope.

Which country and which box language?

Send those two details with your product shortlist, quantity per SKU and accessory requirements. Add the sales channel, sample timing and document questions for a focused discussion with TALRIVO in China.
