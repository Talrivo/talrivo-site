Importer workflow

Six checks before requesting a quotation

This workflow is for pre-order verification. Distributor assortment planning remains covered by the separate distributor page.

1. Destination market

State the country or region, sales channel and buyer type before asking which product direction or document scope applies.

2. Category and model

Choose a focused wired or wireless gaming headset direction, or name the exact audio model being evaluated.

3. Product evidence

Use supplied images and model pages to check the connection type, receiver or cable, microphone, visible controls, accessories and logo area.

4. Sample version

Confirm which version will be sampled and what the team needs to evaluate before branding or bulk-order discussion.

5. Packaging and documents

List the packaging, label, manual language and document questions that apply to the destination market and final version.

6. RFQ scope

Connect the model to the quantity range, sample plan, branding scope, required timing and next purchasing decision.

Importer FAQ

Questions before model and quotation review

What should importers check before selecting a gaming headset?

Check the destination market, sales channel, exact model and connection type, supplied product evidence, sample version, accessories, packaging direction and documents that need to be confirmed.

Can importers compare more than one headset or audio model?

A compact model shortlist can be discussed before samples are confirmed. Keep the categories and use cases focused so each model can be compared against the same buying requirements.

How should an importer ask about product documents?

Name the destination market and final product version, then list the product, battery, labeling, packaging or transport information that needs to be checked. Available documents must be confirmed by model.

When should importers discuss custom logo and packaging?

Confirm the base model and sample version first. Logo placement, color direction, retail packaging, manual language and accessories can then be reviewed against that product.

What information belongs in an importer RFQ?

Include the market, sales channel, selected model or category, intended use, quantity range, sample plan, branding scope, packaging language, timing and documents to check.
