RFQ preparation

What to send before asking for mouse quotation

Model and version

Name the model code and confirm whether the project needs wired, 2.4G, Bluetooth, dual-mode or three-mode connection.

Use case and market

Share whether the product is for gaming accessories, office peripherals, retail bundles, e-commerce listings or distributor assortments.

Specification checks

Confirm sensor, DPI, buttons, wheel type, battery or cable, interface, color direction and any required sample behavior.

Branding and packaging

Discuss logo area, packaging artwork, manual language, barcode area and accessory set after the selected sample version is clear.

Which mouse directions are listed?

Are the current mouse images final product photography?

The current images are PPT-based product references. They are useful for sample selection and can be replaced with higher-resolution studio or lifestyle photos later.
