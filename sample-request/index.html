First inquiry checklist

Information that helps TALRIVO review the request

These fields match the information requested by the TALRIVO contact form and reduce unnecessary follow-up.

Product and model

Name the selected headset model or product direction and explain whether the project needs 2.4G wireless, Bluetooth, wired fallback, RGB styling or B9 Bluetooth headphone review.

Market and sales channel

Share the destination country or region, customer type and whether the product is planned for retail, online sales, distribution or a brand collection.

Quantity and timing

Provide the estimated bulk-order quantity range and when the team needs to evaluate the sample.

Branding and documents

State whether the first sample can use the standard version, then list later logo, packaging, manual language or document requirements.

Sample request FAQ

Questions buyers ask before requesting samples

What information should a gaming headset sample request include?

Include the target market, sales channel, selected headset model, intended use, estimated order quantity, sample timing, branding direction, packaging questions and any product documents that need to be checked.

Can buyers request more than one gaming headset sample from this page?

Yes. Buyers can use this page to prepare sample inquiries for gaming headset models such as G938, G941, G936, G935, G940 and G926 before model-specific follow-up.

Do buyers need an exact model number before requesting a sample?

An exact model number is helpful but not required for the first message. Buyers can start with a focused category and use case, then review a small model shortlist before confirming samples.

Can a buyer discuss more than one sample model?

A compact comparison shortlist can be discussed across selected gaming headset models. The available models and practical sample scope should be confirmed before shipment planning.

When should custom logo and packaging details be discussed?

Confirm the base model and sample version first. Logo placement, color direction, packaging, manual language and accessories can then be reviewed against that product.

How are sample availability and shipping details confirmed?

Sample availability, version, destination and timing must be confirmed for the selected model and project. Buyers should include the destination market and required timing in the inquiry.
