Practical guide

What to prepare

A clear product brief helps TALRIVO reply with a focused model shortlist instead of too many unrelated options.

1. Product category

Tell us whether you need gaming headsets, Bluetooth headphones, TWS earbuds, speakers, Type-C earphones or a mixed audio range.

2. Target market

Share country, sales channel, customer type and any required document expectations.

3. Quantity and price level

Provide estimated order quantity, target price band and whether this is a sample-first project.

4. Branding needs

Clarify logo, package, user manual language, color direction and accessory-set needs.

5. Timing

Share when you need samples, model details, product images, specification files or shipment planning.

FAQ

Common questions

Can I send a short message first?

Yes, but including market, quantity, model interest and branding needs helps TALRIVO reply with more useful options.

Do I need exact model numbers?

No. You can start with a product category and target price level, then TALRIVO can recommend suitable models.

Can one product brief cover multiple categories?

Yes. Mixed audio product review requests are acceptable when the target market, quantity range and product priorities are clear.
