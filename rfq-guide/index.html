Practical guide

What to prepare

Use this page to reduce back-and-forth before quotation, sample selection or OEM/ODM discussion.

1. Product category

Tell us whether you need gaming headsets, Bluetooth headphones, TWS earbuds, speakers, Type-C earphones or a mixed audio range.

2. Target market

Share country, sales channel, customer type and any required document expectations.

3. Quantity and price level

Provide estimated order quantity, target price band and whether this is a sample-first project.

4. Branding needs

Clarify logo, package, user manual language, color direction and accessory-set needs.

5. Timing

Share when you need samples, quotation, product files or shipment planning.

FAQ

Common questions

Can I send a short RFQ first?

Yes, but including market, quantity and branding needs helps TALRIVO reply with more useful options.

Do I need exact model numbers?

No. You can start with a category and target price level, then TALRIVO can recommend models.

Can one RFQ cover multiple categories?

Yes. Mixed audio sourcing requests are acceptable when the target market and quantity range are clear.

Send TALRIVO a clear sourcing brief

Share product category, destination market, quantity range, target price level, branding needs and sample timing. TALRIVO will recommend suitable model directions for review.
