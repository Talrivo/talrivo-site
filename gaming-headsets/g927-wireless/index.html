Questions about G927

Which G927 connection is documented here?

The existing catalogue records 2.4GHz wireless use. The supplied graphics show a USB-A receiver with a USB-C adapter for desktop and laptop hosts. Bluetooth and 3.5mm wired audio remain to be confirmed for the selected version.

Does the USB-C adapter mean every device will work?

No. USB-C describes the connector. Confirm host USB audio support, the selected receiver or adapter, headphone playback and microphone input on the exact device.

Is the G927 package graphic the final packing list?

No. It is a packaging concept showing the headset, receiver and adapter. Agree the exact included set and final artwork for your order. Pictured parts are not automatically included.
