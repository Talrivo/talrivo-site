Product FAQ

Common questions before product review

Is G946 available with and without a charging dock?

Yes. G946 Headset Only does not include a charging dock. G946 with Wireless Charging Dock includes the same headset plus a wireless charging dock. The headset itself is identical in both configurations.

Does the G946 dock charge the headset wirelessly?

Yes. The dock in the G946 dock bundle charges the headset wirelessly. Confirm charging time, dock power input and supplied cables before sample approval.

Can I request a sample of G946?

Yes. Specify Headset Only or with Wireless Charging Dock, then share your destination market, quantity range and branding needs so sample availability and terms can be discussed.

Is G946 suitable for custom gaming headset projects?

Yes. It can be reviewed for logo, color and packaging discussion after the base model and sample configuration are confirmed.
