READ THIS SAME PASSAGE FOR EACH TAKE
"Can you hear me clearly while I play? I am checking this headset at my desk.
Please listen for the beginning and end of each word. Now I am speaking a little
more softly, then returning to my normal voice."

Use your normal pace; about 20 seconds is a guide, not a pass/fail limit. Keep the
speaker, boom position, device, app, input level and processing settings the same
when comparing samples. If something changes, record it and make a separate take.

TAKES TO RECORD
1. Quiet room: read the passage at normal volume.
2. Keyboard and mouse: repeat the passage while typing and clicking as usual.
3. Steady fan: repeat the passage; note fan position and setting.
4. Intended voice-chat setup: repeat with game audio playing and microphone active,
   where supported. Record the selected playback and microphone devices.
5. Mute: confirm whether your voice stops and resumes as expected.

Keep original recordings. Do not add music or edit out a dropout or clipped word.
Review recordings at the same listening volume. Note app processing rather than
presenting an app-processed recording as the raw headset signal.
