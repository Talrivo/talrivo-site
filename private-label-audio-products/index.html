Before artwork approval

Logo on the earcup, or a different headset inside?

A new logo and a new product function are different requests. Separate them in the brief so the sample and development work are clear.

Branding and presentationReview the available logo area, colors, finish, box artwork and manual language against the chosen model.
Functions and hardwareChanges to audio behavior, firmware, structure or electronics need a separate feasibility review with the factory. They are not established by changing the logo.
Approval recordKeep the selected sample version, accessories, approved artwork and document questions together before production confirmation.

Product FAQ

Common questions

Can TALRIVO support private-label gaming headset and audio product projects?

Yes. Buyers can shortlist gaming headsets and related audio products, review samples and then discuss logo, color, packaging, manual language and accessory direction for the selected product version.

Which audio product categories can buyers review for private labeling?

Gaming headsets, Bluetooth headphones, TWS earbuds and Type-C earphones can be reviewed depending on the brand plan and selected model.

What determines MOQ and lead time for private-label gaming headsets?

There is no single MOQ or lead time for every project. Ask about the selected model, logo or color changes, packaging, accessories and any development work. Confirm sample timing and the production schedule separately before committing to a launch date.

What should a private-label audio buyer include in the first RFQ?

Include the destination market, sales channel, product category, model interest, quantity range, target product role, sample timing, branding needs, packaging direction and document questions.

Do I need finished artwork before asking about samples?

No. You can discuss your logo, colors and packaging idea early. Final artwork approval should follow confirmation of the model and sample version, because the branding area, package dimensions, accessories and manual content depend on that version.

Does every private-label audio model have the same customization options?

No. Logo area, color direction, accessories, packaging and available product information should be confirmed for each selected model and project.

Still choosing the headset? Start with one question.

Tell us your target devices, market and what you want to change. You do not need finished artwork or a complete purchasing brief to start the conversation.
