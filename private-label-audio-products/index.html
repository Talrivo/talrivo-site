Project workflow

Move from product idea to a focused private-label RFQ

1. Define the market and channel

Share the destination country, buyer type, sales channel and product role before asking for a broad catalogue or quotation.

2. Choose category and model direction

Explain whether the project needs gaming headsets, TWS earbuds, Bluetooth headphones, Type-C earphones or a focused headset-first range.

3. Review product samples

Confirm appearance, connection, controls, comfort, microphone or audio use, accessories and product finish by category.

4. Prepare branding and quotation

After model review, send quantity range, logo needs, packaging direction, sample timing and destination-market document questions.

Product FAQ

Common questions

Can TALRIVO support private-label gaming accessories and audio product projects?

Yes. Buyers can shortlist gaming headsets and related audio products, review samples and then discuss logo, color, packaging, manual language and accessory direction for the selected product version.

Which audio product categories can buyers review for private labeling?

Gaming headsets, Bluetooth headphones, TWS earbuds and Type-C earphones can be reviewed depending on the brand plan and selected model.

Can buyers compare private-label TWS earbuds and Type-C earphones on one page?

Yes. Buyers can use this page to compare TWS earbuds, Type-C earphones and gaming headsets as part of a focused private-label audio range before sample selection.

What should a private-label audio buyer include in the first RFQ?

Include the destination market, sales channel, product category, model interest, quantity range, target positioning, sample timing, branding needs, packaging direction and document questions.

Should branding and packaging be confirmed before sample approval?

No. The model and sample version should be reviewed first because the logo area, accessories, product information and packaging direction depend on the selected product.

Does every private-label audio model have the same customization options?

No. Logo area, color direction, accessories, packaging and available product information should be confirmed for each selected model and project.
