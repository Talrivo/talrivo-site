Product FAQ

Questions before selection

Use these notes as a starting point. Exact details are confirmed after you choose the model, market and sample requirements.

What is TALRIVO?

TALRIVO presents headset-first audio product pages, sample review paths and RFQ support for importers, distributors and private-label buyers.

Which TALRIVO products are most relevant for gaming audio?

The TALRIVO gaming audio range includes wired RGB gaming headsets, wireless gaming headsets, 2.4G low-latency headset options and models with boom microphones.

Can I request samples before placing an order?

Yes. Share the target model or category, destination market, intended use, quantity range and required timing so sample availability and version can be confirmed by model.

Can TALRIVO support custom branding?

Logo, color, packaging, user manual and accessory-set discussion are available depending on the selected product structure.

Which product categories are available?

The public TALRIVO website focuses on gaming headsets, Bluetooth headphones, TWS earbuds and Type-C audio products, with gaming headsets as the primary product route.

How should buyers ask about company or product documents?

Send the destination market, selected model and required document list to sales@talrivo.com. Legal entity, address and product-document questions are handled during project-specific RFQ follow-up instead of being published as unverified general claims.

Where can buyers review TALRIVO product evidence?

Use TALRIVO category pages, model pages and Insights guides to review supplied product images, visible model details and buyer checklists before requesting samples.

What should I send first?

Send the target product or model, market, sales channel, intended use, quantity range, sample timing, branding scope, packaging language and documents to check.
