About TALRIVO

Audio products shaped for modern listening

TALRIVO is a Guangdong-based consumer audio and electronics manufacturer with over 10 years of production experience. Founded to serve the growing global demand for quality gaming and personal audio products, we operate a dedicated facility with a team of 50-80 specialists across R&D, production, quality control and export coordination.

Our product range covers gaming headsets, wired earphones, true wireless stereo earbuds and portable Bluetooth speakers, designed for clear presentation, everyday use and modern audio collections.

Product FAQ

Common questions before product selection

Use these notes as a starting point. Exact details are confirmed after you choose the model, market and sample requirements.

What is TALRIVO?

TALRIVO is a Guangdong-based audio and consumer electronics product brand focused on gaming headsets, Bluetooth headphones, TWS earbuds, Bluetooth speakers and Type-C audio products.

Which TALRIVO products are most relevant for gaming audio?

The gaming audio range includes wired RGB gaming headsets, wireless gaming headsets, 2.4G low-latency headset options and models with boom microphones.

Can I request samples before placing an order?

Yes. Share your target models, destination market and branding plan, and we will coordinate sample options for review.

Can TALRIVO support custom branding?

Logo, color, packaging, user manual and accessory-set discussion are available depending on the selected product structure.

Which product categories are available?

The collection includes gaming headsets, Bluetooth headphones, TWS earbuds, speakers and Type-C audio products.

Do you provide certification files?

Available documents vary by product. We confirm CE/EMC, RoHS or other applicable records by model during evaluation.

Can you provide product images and specifications?

We can provide model photos, concise specifications and clear product descriptions for review.

What should I send first?

Send target product type, market, quantity range, branding needs, packaging preference, interface requirements and sample timeline.
